What each lane changes

Current
The game today, copied from its code. The body glides at 2 tiles per second and bounces 7 px twice per second at 1×. Walking north or south tilts ±1.5°. A stop eases the bounce out over 120 ms. Turning, stopping and sitting swap the picture instantly.
Approved · 1 + 2 + 3 + 6
Your approved set. A ground shadow at 150%, hop steps with the feet fully planted on each tile, a 3% squash on landing and a 2.5% stretch in the air, at the same 7 px bounce, with no sway. While stopped, the body breathes about 2% every 3.2 seconds. Sitting drops in from 6 px above the seat with a small squash. Each hop covers exactly one tile, so arrival place and time are unchanged.
+ 5 · Rounded corners
Each corner becomes a quarter-circle curve 0.35 tile across, and the character turns to face the new direction 0.3 tile before the corner. The curve takes the same time as the square corner, so arrival is unchanged. It stays inside the corner tile, so it can never cut through a wall.

About this page

It uses your real character stills and the game's own numbers for size, speed and bounce, and it draws the way the game does: whole-pixel positions and sharp pixel scaling.

The chair is a simple stand-in drawn by this page, set to each character's seated pose. The game's chairs are different art. Rooms, crowds and tick timing are missing, so the feel can differ slightly.
