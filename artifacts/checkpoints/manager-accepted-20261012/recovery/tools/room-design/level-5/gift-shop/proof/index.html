Gift Shop

3 × 3 room · One fixed appearance at upgrades 1–5 · Owner review pending. A cohesive shop derived from one full-room concept: joined stocked walls, overlapping merchandise and an integrated checkout/display island around one aisle. No shopkeeper. Necessary furniture isn't removed by doors; optional decor hides for its owned doorway.

The concept sets fixture size and proximity. The playable version uses floor-registered pieces cut or redrawn from it; use S2 to match the concept entrance, and any wall section to inspect another doorway.
