imagegen. Prepaint declared geometry passes 256 door/backing states and 1,344
routes, with all 14 one-tile doors legal and no pass-through exception. Source
hashes are frozen. Empty south meeting chair reuses visitor source.

with real approved seated stills. These are geometry stand-ins, not approved
art or browser screenshots. `prepared/` retains stand-in packing. `replay.mjs`
requires a stand-in build and refuses painted data. Current painted proof is
`../../proof/`.

translation and empty-chair calibration. Occupied seat/floor contacts and
