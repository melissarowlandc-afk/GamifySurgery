in the preview (Room Touch-up Lab → "Wound/Ostomy Clinic (Level 4 design)").

Room: 3×3. A power treatment recliner seen **from the side** (backrest west,
leg rest extended east); the patient sits facing east, the clinician sits on
a stool (reused approved rolling stool) facing west at the leg-rest end, an
exam lamp between them. Sink (reused approved sink) north-west, ostomy
supply shelving north wall, dressing cart south-east, biohazard bin and a
gathered privacy curtain (reused touch-up art) south-west. Wainscot
`#b9c7a5` (sage), clean light vinyl.

## Style and camera

Same elevated-front orthographic camera and painted pixel-art finish as the
approved Minor Procedure / Level 3 art. Clinical and calm. **No wounds, blood,
stomas or body detail are shown**; supplies appear only as packaged boxes.
No logos or readable text. Transparent background, one object per image,
native ≥ 240 px per tile.
