Alerts & Events, rebuilt as the clinic's pulse

Today the panel is one long log. Warnings, jokes and old history share it, so a new joke can push "this patient is about to leave" out of view, and most rows only take you somewhere: fixing the problem is a second click. This proposal splits the panel in two and makes every alert do its job in one click.

  • 1
    "Needs you" sits on top, at most three cards. Only live problems: a patient close to leaving, payroll at risk, trash, an empty cooler, the next build or hire. Each card says what is wrong, why it matters and how long you have. Cards remove themselves when the problem is fixed. Nothing to dismiss.
  • 2
    The button does the thing. "Send founder", "Refill", "Hire · $400", "+1 ad", "Place Waiting Room". Today each of these is a link to another screen plus a second click.
  • 3
    "Around the clinic" below, in time order. The jokes come back at every level, spoken by someone: the water cooler, the printer, the receptionist. Fixed problems drop in here in the past tense ("Trash cleaned"), so old rows never read as current.
  • 4
    You notice it without clicking. The newest line also scrolls across the top bar for a few seconds, live problems get a bobbing pin on the map (click the pin or the card), and the "Needs you" cards stay visible as a tray in Build Mode.
  • 5
    A short end-of-day card. Patients seen, money made, and the review of the day: the funniest one-star line from anyone who walked out. One card per day, never one per walkout.

Fixed regardless of the design

  • Team-discussion answers stop posting "decision recorded" rows.
  • Upgrade requests name the room instead of "The room".
  • "Add cash before payroll" points at money, not the employee screen.
  • "Increase Advertising" only appears when advertising is low and the clinic has room for patients.
  • A save error clears itself once an autosave succeeds.
  • Setup hints stop jumping back to the top every second.
  • Jokes and setup guidance work at Level 3 and above, with new lines for the Reading Room, OR, pharmacy and lab.

Your decisions

  1. Approve the two-part panel: "Needs you" on top, the timeline below. In September you turned down collapsible groups; this keeps one simple timeline and only lifts live problems above it.
  2. Approve one-click actions that spend money (Hire, Upgrade), with the price on the button.
  3. Keep or drop the scrolling top-bar line and the map pins.
  4. Keep or drop the end-of-day card with the review of the day.
  5. Optional: a soft sound when a "Needs you" card appears, off by default.
