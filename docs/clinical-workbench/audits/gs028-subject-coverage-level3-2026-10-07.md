## New narrow meanings and overlap boundaries

1. **Perioperative ART continuity** — `concept.gs028g.hiv-perioperative.art-continuity`. ART continuity and coordinated interruption, rather than antibiotic prophylaxis or selection of an HIV treatment regimen.
2. **Boosted ART and midazolam** — `concept.gs028g.hiv-perioperative.booster-midazolam-interaction`. Route-specific ritonavir/cobicistat–midazolam exposure; not a prohibition of all monitored parenteral sedation.
3. **Quantitative blockade recovery assessment** — `concept.gs028g.neuromuscular-recovery.quantitative-assessment`. Objective recovery measurement, rather than the existing neuromuscular-blockade consciousness/analgesia boundary.
4. **Sugammadex pharmacologic selectivity** — `concept.gs028g.neuromuscular-recovery.sugammadex-selectivity`. Specific relaxant binding, rather than neuromuscular blockade effects or local-anesthetic sodium-channel blockade.
5. **Effluent-associated irritant dermatitis** — `concept.gs028g.peristomal-skin.effluent-irritation`. Skin injury following effluent leakage, rather than high-output fluid loss, stoma prolapse or parastomal hernia.
6. **Correct the leaking ostomy seal** — `concept.gs028g.peristomal-skin.restore-seal`. Correct the leak and compatible skin protection, rather than high-output oral rehydration or prolapse escalation.
7. **Stoma mucocutaneous separation** — `concept.gs028g.mucocutaneous-separation.recognition`. The mucosa–skin junction defect itself, rather than stoma retraction, necrosis or prolapse.
8. **Care of limited superficial stoma separation** — `concept.gs028g.mucocutaneous-separation.superficial-care`. Limited superficial separation with viable bowel and follow-up; exclude deep, infected or ischemic wounds.
9. **Gallbladder lamina-propria invasion** — `concept.gs028g.incidental-gallbladder.t1a-depth`. Gallbladder wall depth and local T category, rather than a gallbladder polyp, incidental stones or bile-leak imaging.
10. **Completely resected incidental T1a gallbladder cancer** — `concept.gs028g.incidental-gallbladder.completely-resected-t1a`. Confirmed completely resected margin-negative T1a disease; exclude T1b/deeper, uncertain depth or positive margins.
11. **Cutaneous neuroendocrine carcinoma recognition** — `concept.gs028g.merkel-cell.integrated-diagnosis`. Integrated cutaneous neuroendocrine pathology, rather than a pigmented-lesion biopsy indication or melanoma metastasis.
12. **Merkel sentinel-node staging** — `concept.gs028g.merkel-cell.clinically-negative-node-staging`. Clinically negative Merkel regional-node staging in an appropriate candidate; no generic skin-biopsy order.
13. **Retrorectus mesh plane** — `concept.gs028g.incisional-hernia.retrorectus-plane`. The rectus/posterior-sheath plane, rather than inguinal vessels or mesh-material choice after enterotomy.
14. **Fascial closure rather than mesh bridging** — `concept.gs028g.incisional-hernia.fascial-closure`. Closable elective midline fascial defect with reinforcement, rather than mesh infection or COPD optimization.
15. **Subtotal bailout in unsafe anatomy** — `concept.gs028g.subtotal-cholecystectomy.safe-bailout`. Avoid division of unidentified biliary structures; subtotal is one accepted bailout, not the sole safe alternative.
16. **Subtotal remnant classification** — `concept.gs028g.subtotal-cholecystectomy.remnant-classification`. Open versus closed gallbladder remnant, rather than postoperative bile-leak localization or ERCP management.
17. **Insufflation CO2 absorption** — `concept.gs028g.laparoscopy-respiratory.co2-absorption`. Absorbed gas and ventilatory load in an evaluated uncomplicated course, rather than venous-return physiology.
18. **Pneumoperitoneum and respiratory compliance** — `concept.gs028g.laparoscopy-respiratory.diaphragm-compliance`. Cephalad diaphragm and respiratory compliance, rather than vascular preload or a fixed ventilation protocol.
19. **Postoperative spinal accessory localization** — `concept.gs028g.postoperative-scapula.spinal-accessory`. Trapezius/accessory pattern after neck surgery, rather than superior-laryngeal voice, median wrist or ulnar elbow patterns.
20. **Postoperative long thoracic localization** — `concept.gs028g.postoperative-scapula.long-thoracic`. Serratus/long-thoracic pattern after axillary surgery, rather than trapezius, thoracodorsal or axillary motor deficits.
