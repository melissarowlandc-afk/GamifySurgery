## Queue-jump policy

After every workbook upload, compare the newest workbook with the last
processed version and re-rank **all** unreviewed concepts. Prefer concepts that:

1. fit the currently playable clinic or near-term Level 0–1 loop;
2. have a narrow, durable teaching point with authoritative support;
3. can produce several original variants without changing clinical meaning;
4. create a useful management decision or coherent multi-decision encounter;
5. do not depend on unbuilt hospital systems or disputed exact numbers.

Later-level and future-hospital concepts remain in the queue, but a stronger
new early-game concept may move ahead of older proposals. Priority is a review
order only; it is not clinical approval or runtime authorization.
