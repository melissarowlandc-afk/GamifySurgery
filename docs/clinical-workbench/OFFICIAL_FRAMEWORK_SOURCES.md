## ABSITE content outline

- Publisher: American Board of Surgery
- Canonical page:
  [General Surgery In-Training Examination (ABSITE) Content Outline](https://www.absurgery.org/resources/exam-content-outlines/general-surgery-in-training-examination-absite-content-outline/)
- Context page:
  [ABS In-Training Examinations](https://www.absurgery.org/get-certified/for-programs/in-training-exams/)
- Version signal: the current-listed document says `Updated 01/2021`
- Retrieved: 2026-07-25
- Retrieved PDF SHA-256:
  `1df9fca970d5d6307b57d76e54f784c4e3763197b851bfcb960d2939354754c5`

The ABS says ABSITE is aligned with the SCORE Curriculum Outline. The outline
organizes coverage into SCORE Patient Care, SCORE Medical Knowledge, and a
small Other group. Its target weights are approximate and must not be treated
as guaranteed exam composition or as automatic runtime patient-generation
weights.

The ABS explicitly states that actual ABSITE examination contents are
copyright-protected and may not be reproduced or disclosed. Do not enter real
ABSITE questions into this project.

The official index says SCORE outlines are updated annually. The PDF URL is
versionless, so every retrieval should store its date, upstream modification
value, and content checksum. Do not label it a named academic-year edition
unless the document itself supplies that label.

An official spreadsheet currently exists at
`https://absprodeus2scorestor.blob.core.windows.net/score/curriculumoutline_gs.xlsx`,
but its available version signals are older than the current PDF. It may be
useful as a structured convenience snapshot, but it must not silently override
the newer PDF.

## Retrieval rules

- Link to official documents; do not commit copies unless redistribution
  rights are confirmed.
- Store the exact retrieved URL, retrieval time, upstream modification signal,
  format, and checksum.
- Keep the stable bibliographic Source separate from each immutable retrieval
  snapshot, and bind every claim to the exact snapshot it used.
- Preserve the source hierarchy as coverage metadata and map project-owned
  Clinical Topics to it.
- Do not treat either outline as a definitive diagnosis list.
- SCORE Portal lessons and other login- or subscription-gated materials are
  separate from the public outline and must not be scraped or redistributed.
