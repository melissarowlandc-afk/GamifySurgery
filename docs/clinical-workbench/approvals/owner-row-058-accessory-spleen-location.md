## Approved scope

Four independent clinic discussions use preoperative counseling, imaging review, hospital-splenectomy planning, and reverse-location recognition to teach the splenic hilum as the most common accessory-spleen location. Each is one shuffled, three-choice decision with no timer or result gate. The action remains discussion surrounding referral for hospital splenectomy; it does not simulate the operation.

## Boundaries and provenance

This approval does not teach an exact prevalence, a complete intraoperative inspection sequence, recurrent hemolysis management, completion splenectomy, or reoperation. A future retained-accessory-tissue pairing remains separately deferred.

The package records Vikse et al. (2017) as the primary targeted-verification source and the verified NCBI Bookshelf StatPearls entry as an independent clinical cross-check. Source metadata remains `needs_clinician_review`; the exact owner-approved content records are `clinically_approved`.
