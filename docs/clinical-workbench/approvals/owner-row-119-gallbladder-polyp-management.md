## Decision

The owner approved one Level 0 clinic management concept:
`concept.gallbladder-polyp.initial-management-category`.

The approved content teaches only three initial categories for six completed
ultrasound profiles: referral for cholecystectomy evaluation, ultrasound
surveillance, or no further follow-up. Every encounter has one scored,
three-choice decision, shuffled answer display, no service request or result
gate, and bounded no-terminal-outcome feedback for each wrong selection.

## Approved profiles and keys

1. Incidental 16 mm polyp without invasion → refer for cholecystectomy
   evaluation.
2. 12 mm polyp with 5 mm adjacent focal wall thickening and no invasion →
   refer for cholecystectomy evaluation.
3. 8 mm pedunculated thick-stalk polyp without focal wall thickening, PSC, or
   attributable symptoms → ultrasound surveillance.
4. 4 mm thin-stalk polyp without suspicious wall change, PSC, or attributable
   symptoms → no further follow-up.
5. Among possible reports for one patient, 7 mm plus 5 mm adjacent focal wall
   thickening warrants cholecystectomy evaluation now.
6. Among possible reports for one patient, 8 mm thick-stalk without wall
   thickening warrants ultrasound surveillance.

## Evidence boundary

The European guideline uses a size-and-risk-factor framework, while SRU/CAR
uses a morphology-led framework; they differ on selected thresholds and
surveillance details. The package retains that disagreement and teaches only
the owner-approved concordant profile categories. It does not combine the
frameworks into a hybrid rule, prescribe surveillance timing, select patients
by age or ethnicity/race, use gallstones or cirrhosis as rules, or add an
ultrasound-ordering or multi-decision pathway.
