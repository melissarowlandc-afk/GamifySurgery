## Boundaries and multistep assessment

- A successful small test supports cautious expansion with continued
  measurement, not immediate unmeasured facility-wide adoption.
- Partial improvement supports adaptation and another linked test.
- No improvement or worse performance supports abandonment or redesign.
- Root-cause analysis and other QI frameworks may be useful for defined
  problems but are not automatic substitutes merely because Study is complete.
- Completion of an intervention is not evidence of improvement.

This package remains single-decision. A coherent multistep QI project should
pair it later with a separately reviewed concept, such as choosing an
appropriate process, outcome, or balancing measure. The same FSRS card will
not be scored twice within one encounter.
