## Approved clinical scope

The concept applies after mechanical obstruction has already been excluded in
an adult with compatible chronic upper-gastrointestinal symptoms. It teaches
four-hour solid-meal gastric emptying scintigraphy as the objective
confirmatory study.

The package does not teach that symptoms alone establish gastroparesis. Upper
endoscopy may help exclude obstruction but does not measure gastric emptying;
esophageal manometry and ambulatory pH monitoring evaluate different
physiologic questions.

Exact abnormal-retention cutoffs, pretest medication instructions, glucose
management protocols, and gastroparesis treatments are outside this approved
concept. Other validated gastric-emptying modalities are not used as false
distractors.
