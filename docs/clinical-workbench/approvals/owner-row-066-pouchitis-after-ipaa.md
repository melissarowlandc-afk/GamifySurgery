## Exact approved versions

### 1A

Patient with ulcerative colitis is considering restorative proctocolectomy with
IPAA. Which pouch-related inflammatory complication should be described as the
most common after this operation?

A. Pouchitis
B. Cuffitis
C. Crohn’s-like disease of the pouch

**Answer: A.** Pouchitis is the most common complication after restorative
proctocolectomy with IPAA for UC.

### 1B

Patient with ulcerative colitis returns for long-term counseling after IPAA.
Which inflammatory pouch disorder is encountered most commonly?

A. Pouchitis
B. Cuffitis
C. Crohn’s-like disease of the pouch

**Answer: A.**

### 1C

A patient with medically refractory ulcerative colitis returns for counseling
after restorative proctocolectomy with IPAA. Among inflammatory disorders
affecting the newly created pouch, which is encountered most commonly?

A. Pouchitis
B. Cuffitis
C. Crohn’s-like disease of the pouch

**Answer: A.**

### 1D

A patient with ulcerative colitis had IPAA several years ago and asks about
recognized long-term inflammatory problems of the reconstruction. Which
pouch-related inflammatory complication is encountered most commonly?

A. Pouchitis
B. Cuffitis
C. Crohn’s-like disease of the pouch

**Answer: A.** This asks frequency only; it does not diagnose the patient or
introduce treatment.
