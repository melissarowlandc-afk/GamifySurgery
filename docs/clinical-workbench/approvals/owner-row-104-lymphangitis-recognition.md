## Decision

The owner approved one Level 0 clinic diagnosis concept:
`concept.lymphangitis.acute-clinical-recognition`.

The concept is limited to recognizing acute lymphangitis from a distal skin
entry site, a tender erythematous streak extending proximally, and tender
regional lymph nodes. It contains five routine-eligible, non-tutorial,
one-decision encounters with three shuffled choices each. Each wrong final
choice has a bounded no-terminal-outcome disposition.

## Approved variants

1. Toe blister with a tender red streak toward tender inguinal nodes → acute
   lymphangitis.
2. Palm cut with a tender line up the forearm and tender axillary nodes → acute
   lymphangitis.
3. Cracked heel with proximal linear erythema and tender groin nodes → acute
   lymphangitis.
4. Reverse finding: a red streak extending toward tender axillary nodes most
   supports lymphatic spread.
5. Finger puncture with a tender red streak and tender axillary nodes → acute
   lymphangitis.

The brief patient presentation precedes a single recognition question in every
case. No result gate, capability requirement, treatment, organism, testing,
probability, exact timeline, or second scored decision is included.
