The discussion appears in the existing Waiting folder with an exclamation mark.
Opening it rechecks availability and reachability, reserves that employee and
walks the founder to an adjacent reachable position. Existing shows travel and
then attention for the question, feedback and summary until filing.

Closing preserves progress; reopening/reloading resumes it. Busy or unreachable
employees defer, missing queued employees cancel, and active participants cannot
be fired. Invalid/orphaned saved reservations are released safely. Each answer
updates FSRS/XP once. Discussions create no patient fee, settlement, clinical
service receipt or satisfaction effect. GLP-1 automation retains its due slot
while the employee is temporarily reserved. Scheduling is an editorial cadence
(first 30 facility minutes, then 180), separate from ordinary patient arrivals.
