## Required scenario boundary

Each scenario identifies biopsy-confirmed pancreatic adenocarcinoma in the
tail, a patient fit for major surgery, no distant metastases, and an explicit
multidisciplinary determination that the tumor is resectable. The question asks
which operation or referral follows **if surgery proceeds**.

The absence of distant metastases is not treated as sufficient proof of
resectability. The variants do not select neoadjuvant versus upfront surgery,
and they do not generalize the splenectomy rule to benign distal lesions,
pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors, trauma, or other non-adenocarcinoma
indications.

## Approved variants

### 1. Clinic operation counseling — active L0

Correct: **Distal pancreatectomy with splenectomy**

- Spleen-preserving distal pancreatectomy
- Pancreaticoduodenectomy, or Whipple procedure
- Total pancreatectomy for the tail location alone

### 2. Procedure by location — deferred Future Hospital OR

Correct: **Pancreatic tail: distal pancreatectomy with splenectomy**

- Pancreatic tail: pancreaticoduodenectomy with splenic preservation
- Pancreatic head: distal pancreatectomy with splenectomy
- Pancreatic tail: total pancreatectomy based solely on location

### 3. Spleen counseling — active L0

Correct: **The oncologic distal resection ordinarily includes splenectomy**

- Spleen preservation is standard for confirmed tail adenocarcinoma
- A Whipple removes the pancreatic tail while preserving the spleen
- Total pancreatectomy is required whenever the spleen is removed

### 4. Select the operative candidate — active L0

Correct: **Fit patient with resectable tail adenocarcinoma and no distant
disease**

- Fit patient with resectable adenocarcinoma confined to the pancreatic head
- Patient with tail adenocarcinoma and established distant liver metastases
- Patient with a selected benign distal lesion being considered for spleen
  preservation

### 5. Referral plan — active L0

Correct: **Refer for oncologic distal pancreatectomy with splenectomy**

- Refer for a Whipple because all pancreatic cancers use the same operation
- Observe without surgical-oncology referral because metastases are absent
- Recommend spleen-preserving resection as the standard adenocarcinoma
  operation

Answer order is shuffled. Names, ages, appearances, and nondecisive details may
vary, but diagnosis, tail location, resectability, operative fitness, answer
meaning, and evidence boundaries must remain frozen.
