## Approved scope

Four single-decision variants teach the clear favorable profile for referral to hospital HPB surgery for hepatic resection evaluation. The active combined encounter first scores the existing, unchanged Milan-criteria decision, then scores this separate resection-selection concept. It has no service timer or result gate; its intermediate Milan decision uses corrective-forward behavior.

The profile requires a solitary HCC, preserved liver function, no clinically significant portal hypertension, adequate future liver remnant, and relevant absence of macrovascular invasion or extrahepatic disease. This approval does not make Child-Pugh A or Milan status alone sufficient, does not present portal hypertension as universally prohibitive, and does not simulate hospital surgery.
