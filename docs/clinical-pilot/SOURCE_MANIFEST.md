# Source manifest

Checked: 2026-07-29

Status: AI-assisted draft; all linked content remains
`needs_clinician_review`. This repository stores only independently written
atomic claims and bibliographic metadata, not source prose, tables, figures,
algorithms, or full text. Medical authority and reuse permission are evaluated
separately.

These sources bound deliberately narrow editorial simulation ranges. They do
not make the generated values diagnostic, define patient-specific normality,
or make an urgent local finding nonurgent. A publicly viewable MedlinePlus
encyclopedia page was considered and rejected because its displayed copyright
terms prohibit AI ingestion.

## Sources used most heavily

The pilot relies most heavily on CDC tetanus guidance for exact tetanus
prophylaxis, the 2022 WSES multi-society SSTI pathway for abscess source
control and escalation, the 2023 JSGE guideline for stable gallstone
evaluation, the 2023 HerniaSurge update for population-limited observation and
acute irreducibility, and the 2025 Swedish appendicitis guideline for
outpatient recognition and urgent evaluation. The two CC BY educational
sources are used only to constrain cross-cutting simulated vital signs.

Restricted sources are metadata or targeted factual cross-checks, not a prose
corpus. Their tables, algorithms, ratings, illustrations, and wording are not
stored or reproduced.
