## Sources used most heavily

The pilot relies most heavily on the CDC tetanus guidance for exact tetanus
prophylaxis decisions; the 2022 WSES multi-society SSTI pathways for abscess
source control and escalation; the 2023 JSGE cholelithiasis guideline for
stable biliary presentation and ultrasound evaluation; the 2023 HerniaSurge
update for population-limited watchful waiting and acute irreducibility; and
the 2025 Swedish appendicitis guideline for primary-care recognition and urgent
hospital evaluation. They provide the clearest direct support for the narrow
concepts selected, and all have an affirmative public or CC BY reuse posture.

Restricted sources are included only as metadata and targeted factual
cross-checks. They are not used as a prose corpus, and their tables,
algorithms, ratings, and wording are not reproduced.
