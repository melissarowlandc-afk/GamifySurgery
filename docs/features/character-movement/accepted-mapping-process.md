## Reuse

Share eight-phase gait timing and contralateral relationships, continuous mesh
deformation, shoe/cuff attachment, pause semantics and validation. Store each
character's measured source bounds, joint anchors, silhouette, garment scale,
handedness and head/neck registration as data. Never copy another identity's
pixel anchors merely because both are human. Preserve source identity and
approved proportions; do not let generated head/body ratio redefine them.

## Per-character preparation and review

1. Pin approved source and source-layer hashes; inspect actual facing labels.
2. Reauthor complete four-view torso/limb surfaces with hidden overlap. Torso
   cores exclude sleeves, heads and any identity hair layer that will overlay.
   Treat atlas grid cells as approximate search windows, never hard crop edges:
   inspect complete connected components beyond a cell boundary. Batch02 South
   shoes/hands crossed y256 and initial grid-clamped bounds cut off real pixels.
3. Retain exact original heads and identity details. Hair over clothing needs
   a tight independent mask, verified visibly without original garment pixels.
4. Measure shoulder cap, anatomical pivot, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle/cuff
   and sole. Use consistent limb width; hands/shoes stay connected and rigid.
5. Fit neutral proportions to the approved source. Use hip-relative shoulder
   height for walking, and verify standing shoulder caps separately. Lateral
   pivots sit at the actual seam, not the high/back edge of the sleeve donor.
   Evaluate visible arm mass after torso occlusion, not scale values alone.
   Use a measured shoulder/chest/hem width envelope when uniform torso scaling
   makes clothing rectangular; deform texture with its surface rather than
   clipping away side wedges. The guide uses the same recorded envelope.
6. Audit each anatomical hand in every direction. Thumb side is anterior;
   donor local reflection may differ by character. Never swap left/right IDs.
   Owner explicitly requires inward-visible thumbs for South walking.
7. In North/South walking BOTH arms always sit behind the torso (owner steering
   2026-09-12 applies to all four examples). In East/West place far arm behind
   legs and near arm in front. South standing caps also sit behind torso.
   Fit head rearward/forward per direction
   while retaining direct neck overlap; guides use the same registration.
8. Review source-only masks and neutral/01/03 assemblies before all 32 frames.
   Then review every phase, full motion and normal scale on light/dark surfaces.
9. Require no folded mesh, detached hands/feet, foreign pixels or neck gaps.
   Connectivity alone does not establish correct anatomy or attractive motion.
   Inspect all full-cycle contact sheets and static crops: faint alpha bridges
   can hide stray outlines from component counts, and nominal source cells may
   include a neighboring character. Preserve original pixels within a measured
   identity region, and register a tight seated crop to its actual center.
10. Record owner acceptance per identity/batch. Preserve accepted reference
    outputs byte-for-byte while expanding to new characters.
    After any profile edit, rebake every affected full cycle before packaging;
    regenerated profiles or mask-audit images do not update staged frames.

## Review image quality

Do not downsample native frames and then enlarge them with pixelated CSS.
Preserve native artwork resolution in the review; if the inline byte limit is
reached, reduce the number of characters shown together before reducing detail.
Keep clean/source/guide registration exact. Inspect crown masks against original
pixels with margin above real hair; a flat crop to hide a stray can remove the
character's authentic head outline.

## Current limits

Standing is fitted; sitting in review remains the approved original pose.
Chair/bed rigging and unusual/nonhuman silhouettes need separate proofs.
Batch 02 tests two approved originals with different proportions and long hair.
Game integration and full-roster conversion are not authorized by pilot review.
