## Disagreement and access limits

JGCA 2025 English CQ3-2 associates opposed actions with the same stated greater-curvature condition. The preceding edition distinguishes the conditions. We record the discrepancy and withhold that hilar-dissection teaching point; no silent correction or inferred recommendation appears in B23. The retained B23 key concerns avoiding routine removal of uninvolved organs. Named clinicians must confirm the complete scope and distractor plausibility.

The direct Wiley Chicago-classification page and JNM publisher page were blocked; the public Chicago author manuscript and bibliographic record were accessible. No reusable license is inferred from PMC. IHI testing-changes pages and the attempted NCI galactorrhea dictionary URL were inaccessible and are not evidence. NIDDK supplies the terminology instead; AHRQ supplies the retained improvement-cycle framework. Incidental search results from a proprietary foregut manual and nonprimary pages were not opened or used.

Existing management-source limitations remain: new ART combination verification does not provide an independent perioperative guideline; the existing NYSDOH continuity/interaction claims are retained. Added ulcer-combination/rescue claims use one directly accessible current guideline, explicitly stated as a limitation. No exact medicine dose, normal-wall cutoff, probability or wait duration was invented. Clinical observations and fictional study values are original scenario data, not real patient observations or claims of actual intervention efficacy.

## B38 endpoint translation

The pre-existing fictional risk-ratio interval is 0.40 to 1.20. Its relative-difference endpoints are calculated as (0.40 - 1) x 100 = -60% and (1.20 - 1) x 100 = +20%. These are arithmetic representations of the same constructed data, not new empirical risks. The existing atomic confidence-interval and risk-ratio claims support the interpretation; the QA receipt independently verifies the conversion.
