## Five added rejection rules

1. **Hide the key; attack the entire visible record.** Reject an item if wording, an announced omission, eligibility labels, purpose clauses or component convergence selects an option before medical interpretation. Examples: the named abandoned rehydration prescription; the budesonide omission; the adrenal question asking what happens before biopsy while offering biopsy; carotid side/timing majority components. Necessary safety negatives stay, without duplicate permission checklists. Record the strongest remaining shortcut and its actual repair, rather than saying cues were checked.
2. **Every distractor must be a credible resident error at this exact stage.** For each alternative, state why a competent resident might choose it on these findings, the particular mistaken inference, and the changed observation that would make it best. Reject an unsupported extreme, categorical delay, already completed test without a new purpose, anatomical mismatch or synonymous alternative. Examples: invasive hemodynamic support in a stable chest-injury patient; lumbar MRI for local groin symptoms; ERCP for gallbladder stones; repeat superficial biopsy after anal-cancer diagnosis. Keep all options in one decision category.
3. **An action key must name the action being decided.** Reject generic specialist assessment, source-control planning or lymphoma-directed therapy as the key when the actual question concerns treatment. Either ask a genuine specialist-selection question or choose a supported concrete test, intervention or treatment sequence. Examples: obstruction nutrition follow-up, mesh sinus, gastric lymphoma and mesenteric referral. Do not turn an unsupported procedure preference into a new key to make the question harder.
4. **Perform a data-ablation and counterfactual decision test.** State the raw discriminator, the inference the learner supplies and the resulting action. Remove the discriminator and check that the same key is no longer compelled; change it plausibly and demonstrate a different best action. Delete decorative detail. Examples: the entire IHC panel was unnecessary for the old family-test key; ARDS fluid-weight narrative did not change the foundational weight rule; anal-cancer hip radiograph did nothing; transplant EBV trend did not distinguish the old biopsy task. More data, rarer terminology and arithmetic do not establish two steps.
5. **Difficulty requires a fair close comparison, not withheld context.** Show decision-critical acquisition, timing, treatment exposure and relevant anatomy as observations. Require two unsupplied inferences leading to a consequential plan. Resolve overlap and verify direct support for the key and the nearest rival before predicting moderate/hard. Examples: fistula anatomy/drainage need; the failed postdiverticulitis examination; chyle-output trajectory; unnamed head-injury rule and missing sampling chronology. If the supported objective remains basic, record that limitation instead of relabelling it hard. No automatic PASS from filled self-check fields.
