## Lessons across the other PASS fields

- A02/A06 and their companions: preserve necessary negatives and actual acquisition details, but do not give an eligibility conclusion. The selected protocol/action changes with the disease compartment or the combination of independent findings.
- B01-04 contexts: a recorded response is different from a valid baseline measurement. Catheter location, repeated-swallow timing and medication exposure can change what a report establishes and which acquisition is needed.
- B05-08/B06-context: separate two symptom generators, actual activity attempted and the site/protocol of previous testing. A completed irrelevant or inadequately acquired study is different from a genuinely adequate negative study.
- B09/B11/B17/B18 and their contexts: interpret the represented target, unsampled target and specimen contents. A positive local result need not classify the entire lesion, and a negative wrong-target sample does not resolve another site. Sequence a consequential procedure around that uncertainty.
- B12-16 and contexts: regional histologic risk is not excluded by a clear scar or negative size-based imaging. Conversely, distinguish missing coverage from an indeterminate covered lesion when selecting the next investigation.
- B19/20 and contexts: trade off target intensity against a biopsy path; compare matching MRI acquisitions before inferring enhancement. More detail is useful only if it changes the selected plan.
- B21/22 contexts: preserve what a specimen does establish while limiting what it does not represent. Their simple management companions are among the five easy exceptions, not evidence that all recognition questions meet the present difficulty target.
- B23/24/B25-context/B30 and context: integrate extent, morphology, architecture and lineage instead of matching one stain or marker; the interpretation controls the care pathway.
- B26-28/B31/B38/39: distinguish the estimand, denominator, null test and protocol target. A decision criterion may be supplied for fairness; applying it must still require interpretation of the actual estimates and uncertainty.
- B32/33/34 contexts and B40/context: the same symptom can lead to a different next procedure because response, anatomy, acquisition or a completed treatment has changed. Every alternative is a real choice at that treatment stage.
- B37/context: recognize off-target tissue and retained suspicious anatomy, then choose the appropriate representative acquisition or retained local-treatment sequence.
- A25/context: connect drug/indication and actual physiologic response to a concrete medication plan; do not replace the difficult interaction with a generic careful-assessment answer.
