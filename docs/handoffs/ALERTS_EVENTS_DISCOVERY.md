## 1. Current implementation

The section is an automatically visible, newest-first message log in the right operations column, below Goals. It has no opening tab or modal. It disappears in Build Mode, including when its own room/build links take the player there. ## 2. Inventory of emit-able kinds

There are three distinct inventories: **domain events**, **condition occurrences**, and **session-only/current-state rows**. Catalog entries are not proof that a live producer exists. The typed domain union has 31 event types; current reducer producers were found for 28. Of those, seven event-type families can reach the board, with most `patience_warning` variants filtered. Nine experience-condition keys and six operational keys exist; low-morale experience history is deliberately filtered. The following tables cover those producer paths and the 37 ambient definitions.

Frequency notation: **D = rolling 600 facility minutes**; complaint categories marked **C** are also separated by at least 60 facility minutes from other complaints. At configured 1x speed, one facility minute is one real second while running: D is about 10 real minutes and the 120-minute humor interval about two real minutes; pause/speed changes alter elapsed wall time (`packages/balance-config/src/prototype-balance.ts:911`). These are simulation settings, not clinical claims. A stable current-state row can be displayed repeatedly without a new emission.
