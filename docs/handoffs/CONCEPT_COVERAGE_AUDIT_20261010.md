Method: inspect the runtime objects and every non-test development-batch module, including family-object exports and aggregate exports. Deduplicate by stable concept ID before counting. Its existing concepts were also checked for collisions when selecting the batch.

The proposed stable ID namespace is `concept.variety3.`. Exact sizes in the cold-snare examples lie within directly sourced recommendations; other numeric clinical schedules, treatment thresholds, probabilities and demographic weights are excluded. Recognition of an external clot does not imply an automatic operation or a fixed time cutoff. Rectus diastasis may coexist with a hernia; the case supplies examination findings rather than asserting that coexistence is impossible.

## Source feasibility and withheld extensions

All ten objectives have adequate targeted sources and can proceed as clinician-review drafts. Current society guidance is paired with independent government/eligible open material where feasible. Small-polyp technique and non-genital hair preparation each have one adequate directly read guideline; their claim records must explicitly state this limit. Government or society patient-education corroboration is not mislabeled as a formal guideline.

Withhold a mandatory same-day/next-day colonoscopy repeat: the ESGE source describes limited/conflicting scheduling evidence. Withhold cefazolin selection, automatic allergy delabeling, challenge protocols, fixed Barrett intervals, fixed hemorrhoid-treatment windows, quantitative risk estimates and universal hair-removal rules for genital surgery. NICE/ASA restricted material and inaccessible ASGE/AGA independent polypectomy content are not used. An access/rights exclusion is not a clinical disagreement.

Sources, complete citations, actual licenses, access dates, claim limitations and exclusions will be recorded in the standalone batch. No proprietary texts, source excerpts or article corpora were stored. All substantive clinical synthesis remains `needs_clinician_review`.
