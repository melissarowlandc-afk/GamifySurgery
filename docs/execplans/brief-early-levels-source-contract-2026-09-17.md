## Roster and nonduplication

Root exported the actual 143-objective release before edits. Rejected duplicate
melanoma, groin hernia, hemorrhoid, PHPT, achalasia and thyroid-nodule candidates.
Selected ten families / twenty objectives below. Generic breast imaging differs
from the new Paget and inflammatory cancer tissue/sequence objectives. AAA differs
from the existing female perioperative-risk association. H. pylori ulcer testing
differs from gastric MALT treatment and acute bleeding hemostasis. First-episode
uncomplicated diverticulitis differs from recovered complicated-disease colonoscopy
and individualized elective resection. EoE differs from reflux, achalasia and Zenker.

1. Adrenal mass: overnight DST for cortisol screening; metanephrines before a
   possible biopsy of an indeterminate mass when tissue would change management.
   Separate visits, not an artificial mandatory DST-to-biopsy sequence.
2. Mammary Paget: full-thickness nipple biopsy; underlying breast imaging after
   confirmation, with the prior imaging status explicit.
3. Inflammatory breast cancer: tissue confirmation; nonmetastatic trimodality
   sequence starting with neoadjuvant therapy, via multidisciplinary referral.
4. AAA: ultrasound screening in men65–75 who ever smoked; elective vascular
   repair evaluation for a returned6-cm fusiform AAA in an acceptable-risk man.
5. Fecal incontinence: conservative stool/bowel-habit care first; endoanal ultrasound
   only for selected sphincter-repair planning in a separate persistent case.
6. Nonbleeding H. pylori ulcer: active-infection testing; planning confirmation
   of eradication after treatment and washout, never pretending weeks passed.
7. First mild diverticulitis: CT confirmation/complication assessment; supported
   outpatient care without routine antibiotics for a carefully selected patient.
8. Thyroglossal duct cyst: ultrasound evaluation including orthotopic thyroid;
   elective Sistrunk referral after benign characterization/no active infection.
9. Rectal prolapse: dynamic defecography when suspected external prolapse cannot
   be reproduced; surgical referral for returned external full-thickness prolapse.
10. EoE: endoscopy with multilevel esophageal biopsies; anti-inflammatory care
    alongside dilation when a symptomatic fibrostenotic narrowing is established.

## Root verified sources and constraints

### AAA

USPSTF. Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm: Screening. December10,2019. Government guidance:
https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/abdominal-aortic-aneurysm-screening
Supports one-time ultrasound screening in men65–75 with smoking history. Use no
prior screening, no aneurysm symptoms, and four appropriate male profiles.

Society for Vascular Surgery. Patients with Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm (AAA).
Live society guideline summary, publicationYear null; accessed September17,2026:
https://vascular.org/node/87
Targeted copyrighted factual verification. Supports vascular referral at diagnosis
and elective repair consideration for acceptable-risk fusiform AAA≥5.5cm. Use6cm
as a clearly qualifying fictional male measurement. Do not choose open vs EVAR
without anatomy; no outpatient waiting for painful/suspected ruptured AAA.

### H. pylori / nonbleeding ulcer

Chey WD, Howden CW, Moss SF, Morgan DR, Greer KB, Grover S, Shah SC. ACG Clinical
Guideline: Treatment of Helicobacter pylori Infection. Am J Gastroenterol.
2024;119:1730–1753. DOI10.14309/ajg.0000000000002968. Official ACG highlight:
https://webfiles.gi.org/links/journals/ACG-Hpylori-Guidelines-Highlights-2024-FINAL.pdf
Targeted society factual verification, no copied table. ACG PUD patient cross-check:
https://gi.org/topics/peptic-ulcer-disease/ (undated live page; corporate author).
Active nonserologic testing and subsequent test of cure are distinct purposes.
Urea breath/stool antigen are valid alternatives; never use one as a wrong answer
against the other. Confirmation is at least4weeks after antibiotics, with PPI
held2weeks; antibiotics/bismuth4weeks. Initial fictional patient is already off
interfering drugs. No bleeding, alarm features, gastric malignancy or acute ulcer
complication. Second node may ask for a future follow-up plan after the positive
test, not claim treatment and washout occurred during the same game visit.

### Diverticulitis

Current ACG2026 primary society highlight verified through the official guideline
index (July2026), not an older-only review:
https://webfiles.gi.org/GuidelineHighlights/Diverticulitis_Guideline_Highlight.pdf
Peery AF, Strate LL, Stollman N, Mankaney G, Chang JW, Grover S. ACG Clinical
Guideline: Colonic Diverticulitis. Am J Gastroenterol.2026;121:1549–1561.
DOI10.14309/ajg.0000000000004047. Targeted copyrighted factual verification.
First-presentation CT characterizes disease and excludes complications. Selected
nonfrail, immunocompetent, stable outpatients tolerating intake, without systemic
inflammation or high-risk CT findings, and with reliable follow-up can avoid routine
antibiotics. Put these selection facts in the case, briefly. CT returns localized
inflammation without collection, free air, obstruction or fistula. Safety-net and
follow-up belong in the selected plan/feedback. Do not imply all patients avoid
antibiotics. Independent source: Peery AF, Shaukat A, Strate LL. AGA Clinical Practice
Update. Gastroenterology.2021;160:906–911.e1. DOI10.1053/j.gastro.2020.09.059;
https://gastro.org/clinical-guidance/medical-management-of-colonic-diverticulitis/

### Thyroglossal duct cyst

University of Iowa, Carver College of Medicine. Thyroglossal Duct Cyst Excision.
Iowa Head and Neck Protocols. May3,2017:
https://iowaprotocols.medicine.uiowa.edu/protocols/thyroglossal-duct-cyst-excision
Academic clinical protocol, copyrighted targeted verification; no technique text
copied. Supports thyroid-tissue assessment before excision, cyst/tract and central
hyoid removal, and treating active infection before surgery. Case must show a
benign cyst and normal orthotopic thyroid on returned ultrasound, no solid component
or suspicious nodes, and a bothersome/recurrent stable mass without active infection.
Do not imply every adult cystic neck mass is benign. Management refers to specialist
Sistrunk evaluation; no procedural steps or invented recurrence percentages.

### EoE

Dellon ES, Muir AB, Katzka DA, Shah SC, Sauer BG, Aceves SS, Furuta GT, Gonsalves N,
Hirano I. ACG Clinical Guideline: Diagnosis and Management of Eosinophilic
Esophagitis. Am J Gastroenterol.2025;120:31–59. DOI10.14309/ajg.0000000000003194.
Official society summary by Swathi Eluri, February2025:
https://gi.org/journals-publications/ebgi/eluri_feb2025/
ACG patient cross-check: https://gi.org/topics/eosinophilic-esophagitis/
Targeted copyrighted factual verification only. Diagnosis uses symptoms, esophageal
biopsies (at least6 from2levels), and exclusion of other causes;≥15eosinophils/hpf
supports the defined context. Adult is stable, no current impaction, and appropriate
initial testing preparation is complete. Returned biopsy may show30eosinophils/hpf
with rings/narrowing and other causes excluded. Anti-inflammatory treatment remains
needed with dilation. PPI, swallowed steroid and dietary approaches can all be
valid; do not falsely force one drug. Refer for combined treatment planning instead.
