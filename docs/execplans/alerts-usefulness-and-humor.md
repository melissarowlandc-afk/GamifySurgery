### Research and design recommendations
These sources document design choices; no claim of controlled engagement data.
- Factorio contextual progression tips:
  https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-361
  Teach the current obstacle when it first becomes relevant; avoid a stream of
  generic tips or requiring clicks to understand the message.
- Factorio causal status and location cues:
  https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-363
  State the problem plainly and make optional navigation reach its cause.
- Two Point Hospital developer account of humor and accessibility:
  https://mcvuk.com/development-news/when-we-made-two-point-hospital/
  Keep a recognizable clinic voice and short ambient jokes between useful news.
- Cities Skylines II citizen/happiness feed:
  https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/citizen-simulation-lifepath
  Personal voices can communicate system causes. Messages should be grounded in
  the actual clinic condition so jokes never imply nonexistent simulation rules.
- Proposed later presentation: subtle one-time new-row highlight, fully readable
  compact copy, stable scroll when reviewing history, no forced acknowledgment.
  Favor problem + consequence + next step; retain single chronological feed.
