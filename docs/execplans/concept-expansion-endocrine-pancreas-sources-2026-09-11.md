## Candidate boundaries and overlap check

- **Asymptomatic confirmed PHPT:** distinct from existing biochemical-confirmation and symptomatic renal/bone surgical-assessment concepts. Scope the patient as biochemically confirmed, without authored stones, fragility fracture, reduced renal function, or another operative criterion. Do not key supplied calcium, eGFR, or urine-calcium numbers as universal cutoffs.
- **Confirmed endogenous Cushing syndrome:** no active Cushing concept was found. The initial node may ask for plasma ACTH to classify cause only after endogenous hypercortisolism is established and exogenous glucocorticoid exposure addressed. A suppressed ACTH result can gate the second node, which selects adrenal CT or MRI. Do not repeat screening or image before etiologic classification.
- **Pancreatic cyst:** no active pancreatic-cyst concept was found. MRI/MRCP duct assessment followed by recognition of an MCN-compatible pattern is the cleaner two-concept pair. Treat body/tail location and absent main-duct communication as supportive pattern features, not a pathologic diagnosis or resection indication. EUS-FNA/cyst fluid analysis is optional only when a result would change management; it need not be forced into this two-node encounter.

## Authoring recommendation

Use two pancreatic concepts: (1) MRI/MRCP to assess duct relationship in a stable, surgically fit patient with an indeterminate cyst; (2) interpret a returned body/tail, noncommunicating cyst as MCN-compatible while choosing specialist review/surveillance rather than claiming cancer or automatic resection. If EUS-FNA is used instead, its stem must state why the result could change management and its returned CEA result must not be framed as proof of malignancy.
