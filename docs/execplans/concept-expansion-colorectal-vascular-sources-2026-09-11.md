## Colon cancer: two additive concepts

Stable IDs: `concept.colon-cancer.preoperative-cross-sectional-staging` and
`concept.colon-cancer.oncologic-regional-resection`.

Start with a referred patient whose colonoscopy already confirmed an invasive,
nonobstructing, nonperforated colon adenocarcinoma. First select staging CT of
chest/abdomen/pelvis with appropriate contrast, then await its result. Returned
imaging excludes distant disease and adjacent-organ invasion. Second asks the
oncologic resection principle: tumor-bearing colon and regional lymphatic
drainage, not local enucleation, routine total colectomy or chemotherapy alone.
Do not assign pathological N stage from CT or imply that negative imaging proves
node negativity. Refer for hospital surgical planning; no clinic colectomy.
Vary tumor site and patient story without testing disputed splenic-flexure
operation preferences. Sheet row56 is inspiration, not an exact adopted key.

## Rectal cancer: response assessment and organ-preservation counseling

Stable IDs: `concept.rectal-cancer.multimodal-response-assessment` and
`concept.rectal-cancer.selected-watch-and-wait`.

Sheet82 is an appropriate candidate only with explicit selection and surveillance
conditions. Start after completion of neoadjuvant therapy for a known mid/low
rectal cancer, with systemic imaging showing no metastases. First order the
combined local assessment: DRE, endoscopic examination and rectal-protocol MRI.
Use an honest external combined assessment gate, not a single MRI that invents
unperformed endoscopy. Return findings compatible with a clinical complete
response. Second asks about organ-preserving care for a fully informed patient
who prefers it, can adhere to frequent follow-up and has access to an experienced
multidisciplinary program. Selected watch-and-wait with intensive surveillance
is reasonable; it is not discharge, proof of pathological cure, or mandatory for
every complete responder. Surgery remains a valid alternative; phrase the
question around the requested nonsurgical option, not the uniquely best overall
management. Do not use near-complete response or biopsy alone as the premise.

## Symptomatic carotid disease: clinic follow-up after acute assessment

Stable IDs: `concept.carotid-stenosis.confirmatory-vascular-imaging` and
`concept.carotid-stenosis.symptomatic-endarterectomy-selection`.

Use a recent resolved ipsilateral retinal/hemispheric ischemic episode already
evaluated urgently by a stroke team, with no disabling deficit, large infarct,
hemorrhage or ongoing symptoms. Appropriate medical prevention is already
started. Duplex suggests severe stenosis; first select confirmatory CTA or MRA
(do not present both equivalent tests as competing answers). The gate returns
severe70-99% NASCET-equivalent ipsilateral stenosis without near-occlusion,
complete occlusion or a prohibitive anatomical issue. Second selects prompt
vascular assessment for CEA plus best medical therapy in a suitable operative
candidate. No elective delay or suggestion of clinic vascular surgery. Use a
clear severe range for both sexes; avoid nuanced50-69% or asymptomatic criteria.

  Public professional guideline, copyrighted, targeted facts only. Supports

  disease. Test choice and surgical candidacy remain individualized.
- `source.board-batch.eso-carotid-2021`: Bonati LH, Kakkos S, et al. European
