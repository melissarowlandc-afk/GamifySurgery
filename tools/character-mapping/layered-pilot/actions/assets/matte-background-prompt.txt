Built-in imagegen background correction after opaque checkerboard output:
Replace EVERY checkerboard background square and all gaps with uniform pure
magenta #FF00FF. Preserve the supplied sprite parts, positions, proportions,
clothing colors and complete silhouettes. Do not resize, redraw or add objects.
The flat matte is removed during deterministic sprite import, retaining every
non-matte RGB byte from the final generated atlas.
