# Indoor Garden — owner design approval

Approved October 11, 2026. Owner, to the Claude manager thread: "Have the plants continuous across the bottom front and the middle front is only empty of plants if the player puts a door there. Otherwise I approve the flower garden and you can move on to the next room." The condition is met in this revision.

Approved state: 4x4 indoor healing garden, one fixed appearance at all upgrade levels. Room-first composition (concept/indoor-garden-concept.png): raised planters and a flowering trellis line the walls, central planted fountain, stone loop path, two inward-facing visitor chairs with seated adults. The south row (S1-S4) is continuous planting; a section opens only when a door is placed there (its planter segment is optional decor for that doorway). Chairs, seated spots and the aisle are necessary and never removed by doors. Manager browser validation PASS (11,040 door/backing states, 103,680 occupied routes, page errors 0).
