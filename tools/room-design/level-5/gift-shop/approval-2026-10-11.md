# Gift Shop — owner design approval

Approved October 11, 2026. Owner, to the Claude manager thread, after the room-first redesign: "Much better." then "I just went to my desktop to look at it. Approved. Next room!"

Approved state: 3x3 self-service gift shop, one fixed appearance at all upgrade levels (only the Founder's Office changes with upgrades). Room-first composition: concept/gift-shop-concept.png drives the playable layout — back wall lined with stocked shelving (plush, mugs, snacks, cards, plants), left wall card racks, flower buckets and flower cooler, right wall plush/snack shelving, central checkout island with flowers, plush and impulse items plus a checkout mat, front-corner balloons and flower buckets, one aisle. No shopkeeper; browsing, checkout and a two-person queue standing spot. Twelve one-tile door sections; necessary furniture (checkout, main displays, standing/queue spots) is never removed by doors; optional decor may hide for its owned doorway. Manager browser validation PASS (3,200 states / 63,480 full-queue routes, page errors 0). Earlier versions preserved under history/.
